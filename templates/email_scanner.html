Detection Engine
What We Check
⏰

Urgency Detection

Scans for high-pressure language like "act now", "24 hours", "suspended" — common fear tactics used in phishing.

🧠

Social Engineering

Detects 6 social engineering categories — Fear, Urgency, Authority, Reward, Curiosity, and Trust manipulation patterns.

🔤

Spelling Mistakes

Checks email body for misspelled common words (e.g. "accoont", "pasword") — a classic indicator of phishing emails.

🔗

Shortened Link Detection

Detects bit.ly, tinyurl, goo.gl and 15+ other URL shorteners embedded in the email body that hide the real destination.

🔘

Button Hidden Links

Finds JavaScript onclick buttons and styled anchor tags used to disguise malicious links as innocent buttons.

🎭

HTML Masked Links

Detects anchor text mismatches (e.g. "Click Here" pointing to evil.xyz) and links where display URL differs from href.

📎

Suspicious Attachments

Flags mentions of .exe, .bat, .js, .vbs, .scr and other dangerous file types commonly used to deliver malware.

👤

Sender Verification

Checks sender domain against 25+ popular brands for impersonation, typosquatting, and free email provider abuse.

🌐

VirusTotal API

Every URL found in the email is checked against 70+ antivirus engines via VirusTotal for known malicious content.

🔵

Google Safe Browsing

All embedded URLs are cross-checked against Google's Safe Browsing database for malware, phishing, and unwanted software.

🔐

SPF / DKIM / DMARC

Parses raw email headers to verify SPF sender authorisation, DKIM cryptographic signature validity, and DMARC domain alignment policy — detects spoofed and forged emails.
